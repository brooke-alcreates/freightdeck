Oversize load holiday restrictions

Most states stop oversize movement on major holidays, and many extend the blackout from noon the day before to noon the day after. Here is which holidays, and where.

The exact window varies: some states stop movement on the holiday only, many from noon the day before through noon the day after, and a few for the whole holiday weekend. Each state page lists its list.

Planning around them

  • Thanksgiving and Christmas are the long ones. Several states treat the Wednesday before Thanksgiving through the Sunday after as blocked, which turns a two-day move into a week.
  • State lines multiply it. A move crossing four states is blocked on the union of their holiday lists.
  • Superloads often lose the whole week. Police escorts and engineering staff are off; a superload scheduled for a holiday week usually slips.

Questions

Can oversize loads move on holidays?

Generally no. 45 states stop oversize movement on Memorial Day, often from noon the day before to noon the day after.

Which states have no holiday restriction on oversize loads?

Kansas, Kentucky, South Dakota publish no fixed blackout list; restrictions are set per permit.
