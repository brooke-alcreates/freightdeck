Frequently Asked Questions

What is FreightDeck?

A heavy-haul quoting tool. Give it your oversize or overweight load and route, and it prices the whole permitted move — permits, escorts, surveys, engineering, linehaul and fuel — across every state on the way.

Who is FreightDeck for?

Brokers, dispatchers, carriers and project-cargo teams who quote oversize and overweight freight and are tired of building every estimate by hand.

How does the heavy haul cost estimator work?

Enter the load dimensions, weight and route. FreightDeck checks each state's rules, works out which permits, escorts and surveys the move triggers, and prices each one in dollars.

What makes FreightDeck more complete than other tools?

Most tools flag what a move needs. FreightDeck prices it — down to police escorts, bucket trucks and engineering at superload size — and adds a starting truck rate on top.

Where does the cost data come from?

Per-state DOT sources, verified and maintained by our team, plus RateAssist's rate model built from real loads.

How accurate is RateAssist?

It's a starting number for most trailer types, built from real loads. Treat it as a first draft — you adjust it to your lane and make the call.

Can I paste a screenshot or spreadsheet instead of typing?

Yes. Drop in a screenshot, a BOL or an Excel project list and FreightDeck reads the pieces for you, even from a messy file.

What counts as a search?

Pricing one load on one route counts as one search. Re-opening a saved quote doesn't use another.

What happens if I run out of searches?

You can keep working with your saved quotes, and upgrade or add searches at any time.

How much does FreightDeck cost?

Plans start at $40 a month. See the pricing page for every plan, including team plans.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes — the oversize cost estimator on the home page is free with no login.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the billing period.
