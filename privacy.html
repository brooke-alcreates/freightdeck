FreightDeck Privacy Policy

Last Update: July 28, 2026

FreightDeck, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, ("FreightDeck," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you entrust to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the FreightDeck software application, website, APIs, and related services (the "Service"). By using the Service, you acknowledge and agree to this Policy. The Service is a business tool for logistics professionals.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — Your name, email address, phone number, user account credentials, company name, password (stored hashed), and company role.
  • Billing information — Any payments are processed by the payment processing company, Stripe. We receive limited billing details (e.g., subscription status, last four digits of credit card, billing country) but do not store your full credit/debit card number. All card data is handled by Stripe.
  • Content you submit — Load details, load dimensions, bills of lading, quotes, and related data.
  • Usage & device data — All actions in the app, rate/permit lookups, log data, IP address operating system, log files, browser/device type, usage statistics, timestamps, and cookies and similar technologies. These are used to help secure and improve the Service.
  • Communications — Any messages, feedback, and support requests you send us.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service and its estimates.
  • Improve the accuracy and performance of the Service.
  • Process subscriptions, billing, payments, and referrals.
  • Authenticate accounts and secure the Service.
  • Provide support and communicate with you regarding your account or the Services.
  • Analyze usage to improve features and accuracy.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Detect fraud and misuse.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

Our Commitment to Customer Data

FreightDeck is a software provider—not a freight broker, carrier, or shipper. Information submitted to FreightDeck belongs to our customers and is used to operate, maintain, improve, and support the Services.

We do not use customer shipment information, load information, pricing information, customer lists, or business activity to compete with our customers or to solicit their customers, carriers, vendors, or business partners.

We do not sell your personal information. We also do not access customer data for marketing purposes except as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain the Service, improve product functionality, investigate technical issues, or comply with applicable law.

Our goal is to help logistics professionals operate more efficiently—not to leverage customer information for competitive advantage.

How We Share Information

We share information only with:

  • Service providers – Companies that help us run the Service, including payment processing (Stripe), cloud hosting and infrastructure, customer support, security, analytics, email delivery, and mapping/mileage and public regulatory data providers. These providers only access information as necessary to perform services on our behalf.
  • Legal & safety — We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect the rights or property of FreightDeck, prevent fraud or unlawful activity, or protect the safety of users or the public.
  • Business transfers — If FreightDeck undergoes a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, customer information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

Product Improvement

To improve our Service, we may analyze your data in aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified form.

Such information may be used to improve routing algorithms, improve estimate accuracy, enhance user experience, develop new software features, or produce statistical analyses that do not identify any individual customer or shipment. We do not publicly disclose customer-specific shipment information without permission.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain your account, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion as described below; some records (e.g., billing) may be retained as required by law. When information is no longer needed or required by law, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted transport, hashed passwords, access controls, and restricted access to production systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices & Rights

You may access or update your account information, cancel your subscription, opt out of certain communication, request a copy of your personal information, and request correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. Any requests must be submitted to us through the contact information provided below.

Cookies & Local Storage

We may use cookies and browser local storage for authentication, preferences, measure usage, analyze product performance, and to keep you signed in and operate the app. You can control cookies through your browser, though some features may not function without them.

Children

The Service is for business use by adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data or personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites or integrate with third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

International Users

If you access the Services from outside the United States, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Any changes will become effective upon posting the revised policy with a new "Last updated" date. If we make material changes, we will let you know via email and/or a prominent notice through our Service and update the "Last Update" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. Your continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Contact
