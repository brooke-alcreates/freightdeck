Puerto Rico Oversize & Overweight Permit Requirements

Puerto Rico legal size and weight limits, escort and pilot car requirements, superload thresholds, and travel restrictions for oversize and overweight loads.

Verified September 2026

In Puerto Rico, any load that exceeds the legal size or weight limits requires an oversize/overweight permit before it moves. Below are the legal limits and the requirements that apply at each threshold.

1When you need a permit

A load is oversize or overweight once it passes any of these:

DimensionPuerto Rico legal limitPermit if over
Width8 ft 6 inWider
Height13 ft 6 inTaller
Gross weight110,000 lbsHeavier
Single axle20,000 lbsHeavier
Tandem axle34,000 lbsHeavier

2Escort & pilot car requirements

Puerto Rico sets escort requirements per permit based on the load and route rather than fixed published thresholds.

3When you can travel

  • Weekend travel allowed within dimensional limits.

4Superloads in Puerto Rico

A load is a superload once it exceeds any of these:

Superload triggerThreshold
Length80 ft
Gross weight110,000 lbs

?Puerto Rico permit FAQ

When do I need an oversize permit in Puerto Rico?

Once a load exceeds any legal limit: 8 ft 6 in wide, 13 ft 6 in high, or 110,000 lbs gross.

Can you drive an oversize load at night in Puerto Rico?

Puerto Rico allows night movement in defined conditions. Weekend travel is allowed within limits.

What counts as a superload in Puerto Rico?

Over 80 ft long, or 110,000 lbs gross.
