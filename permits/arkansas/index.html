Arkansas Oversize & Overweight Permit Requirements

Arkansas legal size and weight limits, escort and pilot car requirements, superload thresholds, and travel restrictions for oversize and overweight loads.

Verified August 2026

In Arkansas, any load that exceeds the legal size or weight limits requires an oversize/overweight permit before it moves. Below are the legal limits and the requirements that apply at each threshold.

1When you need a permit

A load is oversize or overweight once it passes any of these:

DimensionArkansas legal limitPermit if over
Width8 ft 6 inWider
Height14 ftTaller
Length (combination)60 ftLonger
Gross weight80,000 lbsHeavier
Single axle20,000 lbsHeavier
Tandem axle34,000 lbsHeavier

Rear overhang is limited to 15 ft and front overhang to 3 ft before it counts toward length, and any overhang must be flagged.

2Escort & pilot car requirements

Once the load is over…Arkansas requires
12 ft wide1 escort (pilot car)
14 ft wide2 escorts
15 ft highEscort with a height pole / pole car
100 ft long1 escort · 115 ft → 2 escorts
17 ft high or 16 ft wideRoute survey required

3When you can travel

  • Night travel permitted in defined conditions — 24/7 continuous movement (incl. nights/holidays) ONLY for: (1) overweight-only loads within legal width/height/length (27 CAR 111-105(e)); (2) overlength-only combos <=90 ft overall within legal width/height (111-207(c)(2)). Over 90 ft overall = daylight only (111-207(c)(3)). All other permitted loads: daylight hours only (111-106(b))..
  • Weekend travel allowed within dimensional limits.
  • Holiday blackouts: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day.

4Superloads in Arkansas

A load is a superload once it exceeds any of these:

Superload triggerThreshold
Width16 ft 6 in
Height15 ft 6 in
Length100 ft
Gross weight180,000 lbs

Superloads require individual review and roughly 5 days of lead time for approval.

5Permit timing

  • Standard single-trip: ~1 business day to issue.
  • Superload: ~5 days (route survey + review).
  • Validity: a single-trip permit is good for ~5 days.

?Arkansas permit FAQ

When do I need an oversize permit in Arkansas?

Once a load exceeds any legal limit: 8 ft 6 in wide, 14 ft high, 60 ft long (combination), or 80,000 lbs gross.

When do I need a pilot car in Arkansas?

Arkansas requires one escort over 12 ft wide (two over 14 ft), and a height pole over 15 ft high.

Can you drive an oversize load at night in Arkansas?

Arkansas allows night movement in defined conditions (24/7 continuous movement (incl. nights/holidays) ONLY for: (1) overweight-only loads within legal width/height/length (27 CAR 111-105(e)); (2) overlength-only combos up to 90 ft overall within legal width/height (111-207(c)(2)). Over 90 ft overall = daylight only (111-207(c)(3)). All other permitted loads: daylight hours only (111-106(b))). Weekend travel is allowed within limits.

What counts as a superload in Arkansas?

Over 16 ft 6 in wide, 15 ft 6 in high, 100 ft long, or 180,000 lbs gross. It requires individual review and about 5 days of lead time.

How much does a Arkansas oversize permit cost?

The state permit fee depends on the load's size and weight. The full cost of a move also includes escorts, police, route surveys, engineering, and the truck rate, which vary by route. FreightDeck estimates all of it for your exact load — price your Arkansas load with FreightDeck →.

How long does it take to get an oversize permit in Arkansas?

Routine single-trip permits usually issue in about 1 business day; superloads take about 5 days because the route is reviewed individually. A permit is valid for 5 days, so line up every state on the route before applying.

Can oversize loads move on weekends and holidays in Arkansas?

Weekend travel is allowed within the same dimensional limits. Movement stops on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day; many states extend the blackout from noon the day before to noon the day after, so check the permit conditions.

How much overhang is allowed in Arkansas?

Up to 15 ft of rear overhang and 3 ft of front overhang before the load counts as over-length, and any overhang must be flagged by day and lit at night. Past that the combination needs an over-length permit.

Does Arkansas require a route survey?

Yes, once a load is over 17 ft high or 16 ft wide. The survey checks every bridge, sign, signal and wire on the route and is filed with the permit; it usually takes longer than the permit itself, so start it first. What a route survey involves →

Does a Arkansas permit cover Missouri?

No. Every state issues its own permit, and a move crossing into Missouri, Tennessee, or Mississippi needs a separate permit there with that state's escort and travel rules. FreightDeck prices every state on the route in one quote.
