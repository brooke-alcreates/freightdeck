Vermont Oversize & Overweight Permit Requirements

Vermont legal size and weight limits, escort and pilot car requirements, superload thresholds, and travel restrictions for oversize and overweight loads.

Verified August 2026

In Vermont, any load that exceeds the legal size or weight limits requires an oversize/overweight permit before it moves. Below are the legal limits and the requirements that apply at each threshold.

1When you need a permit

A load is oversize or overweight once it passes any of these:

DimensionVermont legal limitPermit if over
Width8 ft 6 inWider
Height13 ft 6 inTaller
Length (combination)75 ftLonger
Gross weight80,000 lbsHeavier
Single axle20,000 lbsHeavier
Tandem axle36,000 lbsHeavier

Rear overhang is limited to 4 ft and front overhang to 3 ft before it counts toward length, and any overhang must be flagged.

2Escort & pilot car requirements

Once the load is over…Vermont requires
12 ft wide1 escort (pilot car)
14 ft wide2 escorts
14 ft highEscort with a height pole / pole car
80 ft long1 escort · 100 ft → 2 escorts
14 ft high or 14 ft wideRoute survey required
14 ft 11 in widePolice escort

3When you can travel

  • Daylight only — 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset.
  • Weekend travel allowed within dimensional limits.
  • Prohibited for OS loads.
  • Holiday blackouts: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas.

4Superloads in Vermont

A load is a superload once it exceeds any of these:

Superload triggerThreshold
Width15 ft
Height14 ft
Length100 ft
Gross weight150,000 lbs

Superloads require individual review and roughly 10 days of lead time for approval.

5Permit timing

  • Standard single-trip: ~2 business days to issue.
  • Superload: ~10 days (route survey + review).
  • Validity: a single-trip permit is good for ~7 days.

?Vermont permit FAQ

When do I need an oversize permit in Vermont?

Once a load exceeds any legal limit: 8 ft 6 in wide, 13 ft 6 in high, 75 ft long (combination), or 80,000 lbs gross.

When do I need a pilot car in Vermont?

Vermont requires one escort over 12 ft wide (two over 14 ft), a height pole over 14 ft high, and a police escort over 14 ft 11 in wide.

Can you drive an oversize load at night in Vermont?

Daylight only, 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset. Prohibited for OS loads. Weekend travel is allowed within limits.

What counts as a superload in Vermont?

Over 15 ft wide, 14 ft high, 100 ft long, or 150,000 lbs gross. It requires individual review and about 10 days of lead time.

How much does a Vermont oversize permit cost?

The state permit fee depends on the load's size and weight. The full cost of a move also includes escorts, police, route surveys, engineering, and the truck rate, which vary by route. FreightDeck estimates all of it for your exact load — price your Vermont load with FreightDeck →.

How long does it take to get an oversize permit in Vermont?

Routine single-trip permits usually issue in about 2 business days; superloads take about 10 days because the route is reviewed individually. A permit is valid for 7 days, so line up every state on the route before applying.

Can oversize loads move on weekends and holidays in Vermont?

Weekend travel is allowed within the same dimensional limits. Movement stops on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas; many states extend the blackout from noon the day before to noon the day after, so check the permit conditions.

How much overhang is allowed in Vermont?

Up to 4 ft of rear overhang and 3 ft of front overhang before the load counts as over-length, and any overhang must be flagged by day and lit at night. Past that the combination needs an over-length permit.

Does Vermont require a route survey?

Yes, once a load is over 14 ft high or 14 ft wide. The survey checks every bridge, sign, signal and wire on the route and is filed with the permit; it usually takes longer than the permit itself, so start it first. What a route survey involves →

Does Vermont have spring thaw weight restrictions?

Yes. Seasonal axle weight limits apply roughly February through May, cutting posted limits by about 25%. Overweight permits on posted roads are limited or unavailable during the window, so schedule heavy deliveries around it. Spring thaw rules by state →

Does a Vermont permit cover New York?

No. Every state issues its own permit, and a move crossing into New York, New Hampshire, or Massachusetts needs a separate permit there with that state's escort and travel rules. FreightDeck prices every state on the route in one quote.
